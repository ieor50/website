Fifty years of IEOR.

Since 1976, IEOR at IIT Bombay has brought industrial engineering and operations research into the same room: understanding how systems work, and how they can work better. In 2026, its people and its next questions come together.

What the Jubilee is for.

The Jubilee puts the work behind IEOR on record, welcomes its alumni back to Powai, and asks what the department should become next.
